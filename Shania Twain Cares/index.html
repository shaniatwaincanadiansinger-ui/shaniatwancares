Founder's Personal Message

"I promised myself that some day I would help kids just like my younger self cope with their disadvantages and to prosper despite those challenges. The Shania Twain Foundation is the next step in my personal efforts to help as many under-served children and youth as I can."

"We will help them find their way in life by providing the support they need to grow into the incredible humans I know them to be. The foundation will provide our community partners funding for tools and resources to lift these kids up with love and compassion and put them on the right track for long term success."
